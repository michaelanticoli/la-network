## OK Los Angeles, Now You Listen to Me

I have made this city my home for the past **13 years**, and it's been an incredibly dynamic and exciting experience. There have been indescribable highs, literal dreams coming true before my eyes, and there have been deep plummets into despair, loss and hopelessness too. It's a full spectrum, whole package deal, and I accept that. I'm not gonna ask the city to change for me, especially when it's allowed me to reinvent and be born again so many times over without prescription.

But I gotta say — this is getting ridiculous.

Why in the world am I still struggling to find work?

*"Your network is your net worth"* and *"it's all who you know"* — well, I've got to send a flare into the sky and ask y'all for help.

## The Situation

I have been striving, trying, gigging, researching, instaworking, selling my belongings, bartering and petitioning to raise funds — earning a little here and there, patching it all together each month to cover the basic bare necessities. I assumed this would be pretty temporary, as it always has been in the past. Finding work hasn't ever been an issue for me, especially in one of the world's and nation's largest cities.

I have applied to **hundreds of positions** across the entire landscape of employment. If there was a role posted and I had relevant experience, I applied.

- **Marketing and Brand Strategy** — my professional background — has been drying up for a while. AI is doing quite a number on that industry.
- **Hospitality, bartending, restaurant work** — my usual bread and butter — has been an extremely disheartening search. A deafening silence that drives me crazy knowing I've sent out my information to hundreds of businesses only to receive nothing back.

**I NEED A JOB.** I will do anything. I am not above janitorial work, food prep, warehouse stocking, or anything else. There is no position beneath me, no role I wouldn't consider. That's where I'm at.

Without a secure income stabilized in the next few weeks, I'm going to be homeless here in CA — forced to decide between keeping my healthcare active and applicable here, or moving into my mother's basement and losing all coverage and sense of self.
